About BR Trading Post

A small UK shop for trading cards and custom painted binders.

BR Trading Post started with a love of trading cards and the fun of finding the card that finishes a binder page. Today we sell Pokémon, One Piece, Disney Lorcana and Yu-Gi-Oh! cards, cards from other trading card games, and custom binders that we paint by hand.

We list everything on eBay, so you can buy with the payment security and buyer protection you already know. This site brings it all together: every listing appears here automatically, and when something sells it stays on the site with a Sold out banner so you can see what we’ve had.

What we sell

  • Single cards for collectors and players, with clear photos and the condition stated on every listing.
  • Sealed product such as booster packs and boxes, when we have it in stock.
  • Custom painted binders, painted by hand and sold through our eBay shop.

Why buy from us

  • Buyer protection. Every purchase goes through eBay, with its secure checkout and protection.
  • Live stock. This site checks our listings every few minutes, so what you see is what we have.
  • Promotions. Look out for offers and giveaways on our promotions page and on social media.

Say hello

Questions about a card, a custom binder idea or anything else? Get in touch, or follow us on social media for new stock and pulls.
