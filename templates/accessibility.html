Accessibility

Last updated: 22 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use the BR Trading Post website. We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What you can do on this website

  • use it with a keyboard, a screen reader or voice control
  • zoom in up to 400% without content being cut off
  • choose a light or dark theme, or follow your device’s setting
  • turn off motion: if your device is set to reduce motion, the site doesn’t animate
  • read every page without JavaScript, apart from search, filters and live updates

How we check it

We test the site with automated accessibility checks on every change, and by hand with a keyboard and screen readers on phones and computers.

Known limitations

  • Product photos and descriptions come from our eBay listings. Each photo’s text alternative is the item’s title, which may not describe everything in the picture.
  • eBay and social media websites have their own accessibility, which we can’t control.
